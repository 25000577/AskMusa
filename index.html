      Why Google Gemini?

      Xcelerate MathsAcademy's AI Tutor uses Google Gemini to provide step-by-step explanations across the supported subjects. Your authenticated request is sent to the Xcelerate backend, which securely handles communication with Gemini.

      Free for every learner

      Every subject, past paper, quiz and AI Tutor chat is open to everyone, with nothing locked and no daily limits. You never need a credit card, a subscription or a payment of any kind to use Xcelerate MathsAcademy.

      About the content

      Every quiz question was written from scratch against the CAPS Grade 12 syllabus. They're not scraped from the internet. The APS calculator uses the standard 7-point achievement scale — always confirm exact rules with each university's official policy.

      Your data

      Accounts are stored locally using SQLite. Your quiz results and tutor conversations never leave your machine. Email verification uses a token emailed to you — no third-party tracking. You own your data completely.

      🤖 Why Ollama?

      Ollama runs the AI model on your own machine — no cloud required. That means your conversations stay private, there's no per-token cost passed on to you, and the tutor works even with a spotty connection. We use llama3 which is strong enough for CAPS-level STEM explanations without needing a GPU.

      💳 Why PayFast?

      PayFast is South Africa's most widely-used payment gateway and supports EFT, card, and SnapScan — things that actually work here. No need for a credit card or a Stripe account. Subscriptions are billed monthly in ZAR and you can cancel any time.

      📚 About the content

      Every quiz question was written from scratch against the CAPS Grade 12 syllabus. They're not scraped from the internet. The APS calculator uses the standard 7-point achievement scale — always confirm exact rules with each university's official policy.

      🔒 Your data

      Accounts are stored locally using SQLite. Your quiz results and tutor conversations never leave your machine. Email verification uses a token emailed to you — no third-party tracking. You own your data completely.
